# Play Store listing

Paste each block into Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing, per language (Manage translations). Limits: app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. Screenshots per language are in `docs/images/play-store/<lang>/`; English is in `docs/images/play-store/`.

## English (United Kingdom) · en-GB

**App name:** Daylock: Plan, Keep, Unlock

**Short description:** Plan tomorrow, keep your word today, and earn back your distracting apps.

**Full description:**

Daylock turns your plans into promises. Each evening, choose a few things you'll really do tomorrow and lock them in. The next day, keep them, and watch the sun rise.

Your distracting apps wait until you've earned them. Pick the apps that pull you away (social media, video, games) and Daylock keeps them closed until your Freedom Time, once today's commitments are kept and tomorrow is planned.

• Plan tomorrow tonight: a short list of commitments, each in an Area of your life
• Lock it in: once committed, tomorrow can't be quietly changed
• Keep your word: tap to keep a commitment, time it with a stopwatch, or excuse it with a real reason
• Earn your Freedom: chosen apps unlock at your Freedom Time when you've kept your word
• Repeat what matters: daily habits, chosen weekdays or every few days, added automatically
• See your Journey: streaks, time invested, Areas that grow, and achievements you're proud of
• Gentle reminders: plan tomorrow, keep today, and a nudge if a stopwatch is left running

Private by design. Your plans stay on your phone. Daylock uses Android's Accessibility service only to notice when an app you selected opens. It never reads your screen, collects browsing history or uploads your app list. App blocking is optional, and you can turn it off at any time.

Available in English, French, Spanish, German and Arabic.
