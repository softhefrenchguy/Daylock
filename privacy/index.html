Daylock

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Daylock is a productivity application developed by Sofien Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how Daylock accesses, stores, and handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Daylock stores information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Daily commitments, tasks, notes, and completion history
  • Routines, Areas, achievements, and progress
  • Stopwatch durations and activity records
  • Freedom settings and selected restricted apps
  • Appearance and notification preferences

This information is stored locally on your device. Daylock does not operate a server that collects or stores your planning information.

2. Accessibility Service

Daylock offers an optional app-restriction feature that uses Android's AccessibilityService. When you enable this feature, Daylock detects the package name of the app currently in the foreground to determine whether a user-selected app should be restricted.

  • It does not read the contents of your screen, messages, or passwords.
  • It does not capture screenshots or record your interactions.
  • It stores your restricted-app selections and the most recent relevant detection locally.
  • It does not transmit foreground-app detection information to Daylock servers.

Accessibility access is optional and can be disabled through Android Settings. Disabling it prevents Daylock from enforcing restrictions through this service.

3. Installed applications

Daylock accesses limited information about launchable applications on your device, such as app names, package identifiers, and icons, to let you choose which apps to restrict. This information is used for Daylock's app-restriction functionality and is not sent to a Daylock-operated server.

4. Notifications

Daylock can send local reminders about your commitments, planning, and Freedom status. Notifications are generated on your device. Daylock does not use a remote push-notification service. You can disable notifications in Android Settings.

5. Backup and device transfer

Android's backup and device-transfer features may include Daylock's locally stored planning information, depending on your device and backup settings. This may involve transferring information to a backup service managed by your device or platform provider.

Daylock excludes stored credentials, restriction diagnostics, and notification state from its configured backup rules. Daylock does not operate or control the Android backup service.

6. Calendar integrations

Daylock contains code for optional Google Calendar and Microsoft Calendar integrations, but these integrations are not enabled in the current release. If enabled in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant account access, calendar information, and data transfers.

7. Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The current version of Daylock does not include advertising, analytics tracking, or a third-party crash-reporting service. Daylock does not sell your personal information.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your planning information remains stored on your device until you remove it using available app controls, clear Daylock's application data, or uninstall the application. Android backups may retain previously backed-up information according to your device and backup-provider settings. Uninstalling Daylock may not automatically delete those backups.

9. Security

Daylock uses Android's local application storage mechanisms. Sensitive credentials, where applicable, use Android Keystore-backed secure storage. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

Daylock is a general productivity application and is not specifically designed for children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Daylock's features or data practices change. The updated policy will display a revised effective date.
